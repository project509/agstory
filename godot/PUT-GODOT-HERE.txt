Drop a Godot 4 Windows executable in this folder to make play.cmd self-contained.

  1. https://godotengine.org/download/windows/  (Godot 4.7, standard build)
  2. unzip; put Godot_v4.7.x-stable_win64.exe here (the _console.exe too if you like)
  3. zip the whole project folder and send it; play.cmd finds it first

The .gdignore file beside this note keeps Godot's importer from scanning the
executable, and .gitignore keeps the 100+ MB binary out of the repository.
