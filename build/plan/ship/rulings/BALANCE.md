### #1b — `Loot.rolls_for` (Adventures) / docs/10 §4.2, §11 — how long the farm takes

**Ruling:** An Adventure rung rolls **two items per declared loot slot** (`Loot.ADVENTURE_ROLLS_PER_SLOT := 2` → A1 4, A2 2, A3 4), keeping BL-31's upgrade bias; the raid's `RAID_ROLLS` (2/2/2/3/3) are untouched.

**Inspiration:** In It's A Wipe! the easy content is the farm and the raid is the wall; the farm has to be short enough that the player reaches the wall while the joke is still fresh. The designer's Adventure hands out *the tier's set* — the ideaboard's "Tier 1 Adventure gear" is a full four-piece set per family plus weapons and charms, assigned to the Adventure, not to an encounter.

**Canon:** *"Tier 1 Adventure gear in screenshots"* (raw notes) and ideaboard §2 — the Adventure's loot is the set. docs/10 §4.2's `rolls(encounter) = E1:2 …` prices the raid's scarcity; Adventures were never given a roll count and the tree fell back to `loot_slots.size()`. docs/11 §4.3 budgets *"~9 mission attempts"* per tier; at one item per slot per clear, dressing the raid's twelve from A1-A3 costs 36 clears (12 feet + 12 weapons over 2 rolls, 12 legs over 1, 24 over 2) — four times the tier's budget. At two it is 18.

**Cost:** `sim/core/Loot.gd` one constant; `tests/unit/test_loot.gd` (or the loot half of `test_raid_sim.gd`) one assertion; docs/10 §4.2 one line. **Register row:** BL-NEXT — *An Adventure rung rolls two items per loot slot* (DECIDED — implemented). docs/10 §4.2 counts the raid's rolls and says nothing of the Adventure's; the tree rolled one per slot, which made the set the raid demands (docs/08 §9.2 enters Raid 1 in full Adventure gear) a 36-clear farm. `Loot.ADVENTURE_ROLLS_PER_SLOT = 2` halves it to 18, inside docs/11 §4.3's first town cycle, and BL-31's upgrade bias keeps the extra rolls from becoming a gold faucet until every raider is dressed — ruled by the loop under the designer's 2026-09-15 delegation.
