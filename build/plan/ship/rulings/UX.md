# UX batch — rulings

Ruled by the loop under the designer's 2026-09-15 delegation. Rows: §6 #6 #34 #36 #38 #39 #51 #60 #64; §7.1 "S13 encounter interstitial, S16 Codex, `nav_codex`", "A 'Raid Group' read-only view (Q18j / HALL-21)", "A soft-lock bail-out (a free Common, a loan)".

The one commit rule this batch states once and #6, #36 and #51 all obey: **the attempt is committed at Depart.** The sim resolves in full and `GameState.record_attempt()` autosaves before RaidView plays a line (`RaidView.gd:399-408`). Everything after Depart — speed, pause, skip, Esc, quitting, reloading, leaving by either exit — is presentation, and none of it can change, discard or reroll the record. There is no mid-fight verb (Q-09, binding), so there is no Retreat, no attempt counter and nothing to protect with a lock.

## Rulings

### #60 — LOOP q9 / SHIP q8 / SHIP-13 / LOOP-25 — disband recoverable; the soft-lock bail-out

**Ruling:** Disband is recoverable (the roster is wiped; gold, buildings, unlocks and rank are kept; RP −10% within the rank, clamped to the floor — Q-10's fourth option as built in `Reputation.rp_after_disband`), and the ship default's "no bail-out" is overturned: a guild that cannot field the next rung and cannot afford the cheapest seat on the Tavern board gets **the walk-in** — one Common on the board at 0 G, tagged "Will raid for a bed.", one per board refresh for as long as the condition holds; no loan.

**Inspiration:** Q-10's own reasoning for "recoverable" is that a run-ending disband makes players refuse to field low-morale raiders, and the bring-Steve-anyway decision canon calls "very appropriate for the guild-leader fantasy" collapses. A disbanded guild whose only door is New Guild is a run reset by another name, so the default contradicts the ruling it sits under. The walk-in is the designer's own world: "@Rank unknown you can only find the worst players to join your guild, they have 0 raid experience" — the guild leader whose tent is empty and whose purse is light gets exactly what the tagline promises, questionable people, and the only one who turns up is the one who will work for a bed. *It's A Wipe!* never lets the guild actually die; there is always an applicant. A loan is refused — there is no ledger of debt in the game, it would add a repayment mechanic and a field to a frozen save, and a guild in hock is not funny in the way a walk-in is.

**Canon:** Raw notes, Morale table: "0-10 Very Upset — may cause guild disband" (canon's only loss condition); docs/00 A6 "No permadeath of the save, no roguelike run wipe"; docs/01 §6.3 rejects "Run reset / roguelike restart" because "a town you lose is a run, not an engine"; docs/03 §6.5 "rank never decreases"; docs/05 §6.3's five guarantees (two ticks of warning, the strike-2 modal, the number, no silent state, autosave before the roll) — W8-CRISIS builds them as planned. docs/03 §8.1 M1 ("every Tavern refresh guarantees at least one recruit at the rank's modal tier") is the seam the walk-in uses: the guarantee stands, and the walk-in is a sixth card, always a Common, priced 0 — the canon find-rule ("@Respected you no longer find common") describes who the guild *finds*; the walk-in finds the guild, and the card says so.

**Cost:** W8-CRISIS at its L size (the plan sized it: "L only if the page bought the solvency floor"). The predicate moves into the state: `GameState.is_insolvent() -> bool` = `roster.size() < RaidPlan.party_size(next_open_mission)` AND `gold < cheapest seat on tavern_board` (`Recruitment.cost_of` under whichever `PRICE_SCALE` — 15 G for a Common at doc 11's, 60 G at doc 04's; at Respected+ the cheapest seat is an Uncommon/Rare, which is exactly where the lock bites) AND `Economy.total_sell_price(pending_loot + worn gear, rank) < that seat`; `tools/playtest.gd:_can_still_make_progress` becomes a call to it (one owner). `refresh_board()` passes `"walk_in": is_insolvent()` into `Recruitment.roll_board`, which appends one Common candidate with `walk_in = true` beyond `board_slots()`; `cost_of` is unchanged and `hire()` charges 0 when `who.walk_in`; the candidate dict carries `walk_in` in `to_dict()`/`from_dict()` (a key inside `tavern_board[]` entries with a default on read — not a top-level key, so W7-SAVE's `FROZEN_SHAPE` holds). Tavern card: price "Free", quote line "A walk-in. Will raid for a bed."; the hire sentence "Hiring <name> leaves <gold>" needs no change. Board sentence (SHIP-13's, reworded): "You cannot field a party and cannot afford a recruit. Someone at the Tavern will raid for a bed." — printed by `AdventureBoard` when `is_insolvent()`. Post-disband Town callout, from `flags.disbanded_day`: "The guild disbanded on day N. The tent is still yours, and so is the purse. The Tavern is up the road." with one action, the Tavern. Tests: `test_game_state.gd` constructs roster 0 / gold 5 / empty inventory and asserts the board carries one 0 G Common, that hiring it costs nothing, and that a solvent guild's board carries none; `test_full_loop.gd`: `_can_still_make_progress()` is green after a disband. **Register row:** Q-10 rider / 00-plan §6 #60 — RULED (2026-09-15).
