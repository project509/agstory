### #61 — LOOP q10 / LOOP-12 / CRITIC-C12 — Who triggered the wipe, and the −4

**Ruling:** Keep the rule as W6-SIM-CASCADE built it — the culprit is the last Severe-or-worse mistake logged before the first tank or healer death, else the deepest live cascade (ties to the later one), else nobody — and the extra morale delta to that one raider is −4, `RaidSim.WIPE_CULPRIT_DELTA = -4` = `Morale.TRIGGERS["wipe_caused"]`, once per attempt session.

**Inspiration:** *It's A Wipe!*'s wipe report is a blame screen; the comedy is that one person's name is on it. Pillar 5's build test (docs/00 §3): "after any wipe, the post-raid screen can name the raider, the mistake, and the town action that would have prevented it." A rule that blames the mistake the fight never recovered from (the one before the tank or healer went down) is the one a guild leader would recognise; "nobody — the boss simply won" is the honest line when the raid was simply too weak, and it is also the DPS-check signal the town loop needs.

**Canon:** docs/01 §6.1 (🔷): "Morale −4 to every participant; an extra −4 to the raider whose mistake triggered the wipe" — the NUMBER is the docs'; the RULE was nowhere. docs/07 §6's cascade graph ("The post-mortem screen reads the `caused_by` graph and renders roots + longest chain") is what clause 2 reads. Nothing in docs/_source speaks to it.

**Cost:** none new — `RaidSim._wipe_cause()` and `_log_wipe_cause()` are in the tree (wave 6), `SimResult.wipe_cause` carries {actor_id, mistake_type, round, entry_seq}, `deltas_queued[*].wipe_caused` marks the one raider and `game/` applies the −4 through the ledger. W7-REPORT's sentence under the stamp reads the field ("Round 21: Steve stood in the fire. Everything after that was arithmetic."). Balance effect: zero on the fight; on the town loop the culprit takes −12 instead of −8 on a wipe (docs/05 §7.1's −8 plus this −4). BALANCE's #62 sits beside this row — see Tensions.

**Register row:** BL-NEW-A (next free id; docs/15 runs to BL-109) — The wipe's culprit is a rule, not a guess. `RaidSim._wipe_cause()` names the last Severe-or-worse mistake logged before the first tank or healer is confirmed Dead; if no tank or healer died, the mistake with the deepest cascade depth (ties to the later one); if there was no cascade, nobody — the boss simply won, and the report says so instead of inventing a name. The named raider takes docs/01 §6.1's extra −4 (`WIPE_CULPRIT_DELTA`, `Morale.TRIGGERS["wipe_caused"]`, once per attempt session) on top of the wipe's own delta, and the log's last Story line on a loss says "It traces back to …" so the goldens carry the verdict. ### #56 — SIM q8 / SIM-12 — Attrition grace after the enrage round

**Ruling:** Wipe-by-attrition fires at `enrage_round + attrition_grace` with the grace **5 rounds** — the number every clear rate in `tests/baselines/sweep_baseline.csv` was measured under, promoted from an unstated `+ 5` to `RaidSim.ATTRITION_GRACE_ROUNDS := 5`, overridable per encounter as `attrition_grace` in the record — and the log announces the enrage at `enrage_round` on every fight so a loss on the clock reads as a countdown, not a crash; the 40-round cap stays the safety.

**Inspiration:** the enrage timer is the genre's one legible DPS check — *It's A Wipe!* shows it counting down, and "we didn't have the damage" is the report that sends a guild leader to the Market. With Focus out of 1.0 (#54) healing is unlimited, so the enrage clock is the ONLY thing that stops a stable-but-weak raid grinding any fight to a win over 35 rounds; the ship default ("none", the cap only) deletes the DPS check from seven of Tier 1's eight fights and from every Adventure. Gear must stay "the whole progression story" (Formulas.gd's own BL-27 note), which needs a fight that can be lost for lack of it.

**Canon:** docs/_source is silent on enrage. docs/10 §5.2 defines `enrage_round = ceil(1.4 × target_rounds)` on every record and every §7 card's Fail line reads "All 12 dead, or enrage at round N"; docs/10 §6 says "enrage expiry"; docs/07 §7.3 says the 40-round cap is a safety property. The baseline (A1 starting/common/55: 57 of 200 outcomes are attrition; E5 raid_entry: 39 of 200) shows the clock is a live lever, not a corner case.

**Cost:** W8-SIM-BALANCE: `_check_end` reads `encounter.attrition_grace` (default `ATTRITION_GRACE_ROUNDS`, validator `>= 0`), one STORY line at `enrage_round` on fights without an authored M06 — "Round 17. Five rounds before this stops being a fight." — and `test_raid_sim.gd::test_attrition_fires_at_the_enrage_round_plus_the_grace`; `Encounter.gd` gains the optional key. Balance effect at the default: none (the tree already runs +5). The per-encounter key is BALANCE's lever for the A-rung (#1/#9): A1-A3 may carry a longer grace if their curve needs it, without a sim-wide rule change. docs/07 §7.3's table gains the row; docs/10 §6's "enrage expiry" gets the number.

**Register row:** BL-NEW-B (next free id) — Wipe by attrition fires at the encounter's `enrage_round` plus a grace, and the grace is a number: `RaidSim.ATTRITION_GRACE_ROUNDS := 5`, the value the tree has run since the mech-arms unit and the one every baseline clear rate was measured under, now named and overridable per record as `attrition_grace`. ### #55 — SIM q4, q5 / SIM-13 / SIM-27 #2 — E4's three mechanics get their numbers

**Ruling:** E4's M05 carries `effect: {"kind": "raid_damage", "amount": 27}` (the tier's M02 pulse) on `round 4, every 5`; M08 Fixate is a window, `{"rounds": 2, "every": 6}`; M09 Healing Debuff recurs, `{"reduction_pct": 40, "rounds": 3, "round": 3, "every": 6, "target": "active_tank"}` — the recommendations, with M08's period moved 5 → 6 so the fixate never overlaps the debuff it would otherwise make decorative.

**Inspiration:** *It's A Wipe!*'s fourth boss is where a raid learns it is a portfolio; docs/10 §7.4 says so in its own design note. An interrupt check "in a game where you do not press the button" is only a joke if the uninterrupted cast hurts; a fixate is only a story if the boss comes BACK; a tank healing debuff only bites while the boss is on the tank. Three mechanics that visibly take turns — debuff on the tank rounds 3-5, boss runs off rounds 6-7, one clean round, repeat — is a fight the player can read coming, which docs/07 §8.2 calls "most of the entertainment".

**Canon:** docs/_source gives E4 "Encounter 4 (Mini boss) — Chest + strong weapons — ★★★★" and nothing else. docs/10 §10 M05 "effect `E` fires" (E never named; BL-83 makes it per-encounter data); docs/10 §7.4 "M08 Fixate (5 rounds)" authored as a permanent fixate by accident (SIM-13); handoff-mech-arms #2 asked for M09 `round 3, every 6` and was never applied. The 27 is docs/08 §9.5's `AOE_BITE_FRACTION × cloth_max_hp` as the generator already computes it for E3 (E2 26, E3 27, E5 28).

**Cost:** W8-SIM-BALANCE's content pass: three params in `data/encounters_t1.json`; `Encounter.validate()` gains "an M05 spec carries `effect.amount > 0`" and "an M08 `rounds` is strictly less than its `every`"; the E4 golden `e4_rares_raid_entry` (SIM-16) pins all three; docs/10 §7.4's card and §10's M05 row name the shape. W9-TIERS applies the same three rules through the generator to t2_e4, t4_e3, t5_e4's M05 and the tier 2-5 M08/M09 carriers. The per-6 cycle over 17 rounds: M09 live 3-5 / 9-11 / 15-17, M08 6-7 / 12-13, M05 checks at 4 / 9 / 14 — zero fixate-under-debuff rounds (the `every 5` reading had five). Balance effect: E4 falls from its measured 200/200 at Common/raid_entry/55; the star buys teeth. BALANCE's #9 curve should set E4 below 100 — see Tensions.

**Register row:** BL-NEW-C (next free id) — E4's mechanics are authored, not implied. ### #58 — CONTENT q5 / CONTENT-12 / SIM-15 — MIS_NINJAPULL: build the break, or cut it

**Ruling:** Build it — the break is the moment between two pulls of the same raid in one town cycle, `GameState.start_attempt` rolls it at Depart (one `Mistakes.roll` per living party member at the AMBIENT site with `ctx.break_phase = true`; only a MIS_NINJAPULL draw counts; the first hit in slot order pulls, at most one per break), and `RaidSim.run()` honours `mstate.ninja_pulled` by starting the fight with the provisions unapplied and unconsumed, the mistake logged at round 1 at its base Critical band with its Distraction token live; the eight lines stay and the type is never flagged unreachable.

**Inspiration:** "Questionable people. Worse decisions." Somebody pulling the boss during the break is the raid-guild joke — it is the one mistake every player who has ever raided has a story about, and *It's A Wipe!* lives on exactly this between-pull idiocy. The corpus's eight lines are among its best ("{actor} stood up to stretch and pulled the boss on the way."; "{actor} tested one thing to see what it did, and it did all of this.") and a Critical at round 1 is the perfect culprit for #61's rule: "It traces back to Greg — Ninja-Pulled During the Break, round 1." Cutting a type to save an afternoon is the wrong trade in a comedy game.

**Canon:** docs/_source describes the ladder as "raid raid, adventure adventure" and never a break; docs/07 §4.1 Phase 6 lists "ninja-pull during a break" among the ambient rolls and §5.2 row 14 gives the consequence: "Next encounter starts immediately: consumables not applied, resources not restored, Calls still on cooldown" — the last two halves are moot (no Focus in 1.0, Q-09 no Calls). Q-52 runs one encounter per attempt, so the break lives in `game/`, which keeps `sim/` pure (docs/14 §3.1) and needs one dict key on the way in.

**Cost:** W8-CRISIS (owns `GameState.gd`): the Depart roll on a new seeded channel `break` (derived from the attempt seed like the others), fired when the mission already has an attempt this cycle — a clear OR a wipe is a break; A1-A3 and the tutorials have none (single encounter; NINJAPULL is in `TUTORIAL_DISABLED_TYPES`). W8-SIM-BALANCE (owns `RaidSim.gd`): `run()` reads `mstate.ninja_pulled` (a raider id), skips the provisions pass and lists every item in `result.consumables_unspent` with reason `ninja_pulled` (nothing is consumed — the potions are still in the bag), emits the entry through `_log_mistake` at round 1 ROUND_OPEN with `Token.DISTRACTION` (+5pp raid-wide, 2 rounds) and a line from the type's own variants. `Mistakes.gd` unchanged (`requires: break_phase` is now true somewhere); W7-SIM-EFFECTS does NOT add `NINJAPULL_REACHABLE`. Tests: `test_mistakes.gd`'s two hand-set break tests stand; `test_raid_sim.gd::test_a_ninja_pull_starts_the_fight_unbuffed_and_names_the_puller`; `test_game_state.gd::test_the_break_rolls_only_after_a_first_pull`; the E4 golden gains nothing (goldens call the sim directly). Expected rate per break with NINJAPULL's ambient share (weight 5 of AFK 8 + ARGUMENT 7 + NINJAPULL 5 + NO_CONSUMABLE 6): a full Common party at Content ≈ 48 %, Rare ≈ 21 %, Epic ≈ 9 %, Legendary ≈ 3 %, ×2 weight at Annoyed or worse — the worst guild pulls early every other break, and it stops as the roster improves. Balance effect: none on the sweep (`balance_sweep.gd` calls the sim, not the break); in play a Common raid loses its provisions on roughly half its E2-E5 pulls — BALANCE's curve is measured without it, stated in Tensions.

**Register row:** BL-NEW-D (next free id) — The break phase exists, in `game/`. When the player presses Depart on a raid encounter and the mission already has an attempt this town cycle (a clear or a wipe — either is a break), `GameState.start_attempt` rolls one ambient check per living party member on the seeded `break` channel with `break_phase` true; only a MIS_NINJAPULL draw counts, the first in slot order pulls, at most one per break. The sim receives `ninja_pulled` and starts the fight with the provisions unapplied and unconsumed (`result.consumables_unspent`, reason `ninja_pulled`), the mistake on the record at round 1 at its Critical band with its Distraction token live. Adventures, the tutorials and a raid's first pull have no break. Pinned by `test_raid_sim.gd::test_a_ninja_pull_starts_the_fight_unbuffed_and_names_the_puller` and `test_game_state.gd::test_the_break_rolls_only_after_a_first_pull` — ruled by the loop under the designer's 2026-09-15 delegation.
