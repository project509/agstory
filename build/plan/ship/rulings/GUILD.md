### #12 — DESIGNER-12 / M5-END-5 — Legendary 5% at Legendary rank: accept or retime

**Ruling:** Accept — the 9-of-9 Legendary collection is the endgame; the 5% find rate at Legendary rank and the 3200 RP threshold stay where they are.

**Inspiration:** *It's A Wipe!* ends when its content ends and lets you keep raiding; what you have at the end is the roster you built. The designer's own endgame line is the Legendaries — "You can only ever find 1 Legendary per class - They are also named characters - IE Natsuna(the shaman)" — nine named people to find, one per class, is the guild-leader's trophy case, the same shape as Octopath's eight travellers: the game is done when the party is. Retiming the rate earlier would spend the prize on a rank that still has raids to gate, which is the opposite of what a prestige rank is for.

**Canon:** raw notes, Guild Reputation: "you also gain a VERY SMALL chance to find raiders that are basically perfect … (You can only ever find 1 Legendary per class". DECIDED rows: docs/15 Q-23 "(b) Prestige. Revisit when post-Raid-5 content is designed"; Q-88 "the save continues: Legendary collection to 9-of-9, achievements, flat repeat clears. No Tier 6 in 1.0"; docs/10 §13 row 2 names the collection as the continuing activity and row 4 forbids a Tier 6. docs/03 §6.4's pacing ("Legendary rank lands on the full clear of Raid 5") is pinned by `tests/unit/test_reputation.gd` `test_the_pacing_table_reproduces_row_by_row` and `test_the_pacing_contract_holds_one_rank_per_tier_at_boss_three_or_four`; retiming re-solves that table for a rank whose only content is the Tavern.

**Cost:** No number moves: `data/reputation.json` legendary `find_weights [0,0,0,950,50]` and `sim/core/Reputation.gd` `THRESHOLDS[5] = 3200` stay; `Recruitment.gd`'s pity (`pity_threshold` 25 on Rare-or-better, §8.1 M2) stays. docs/03 §5.4 gains one sentence ("The 5% is the collection's rate, not a content gate: nine Legendaries at 5% per candidate is ≈180 generated candidates, ≈30 Tavern boards at six a board — the endgame's length, by design"); docs/10 §13 row 3 closes "accepted"; a BL row. ### #13 — DESIGNER-13 / M5-QAB-4 / Q-69 — does the achievement board pay reputation

**Ruling:** Yes — the board's two `reputation`-kind records ("One of Each", "Comfortable") pay **15 RP** each, the `achievement_rp` switch ships **on**, and board RP is live-capped at 15% of RP earned exactly the way the board's coin already is.

**Inspiration:** The designer's Guildhall is four things to do — train, comfort, upgrade, and the "Quest/achievement board" — inside a town that is "your progression engine" rather than "a menu between raids". A board that pays only coin is a menu button with a purse behind it; a board the town *hears* is part of the engine. Pillar 1's build test ("for every reward in the game, name the building it changes") passes for reputation and fails for 40 G. The two records that carry the kind are the two that are about the guild as a guild — every class on the books, a furnished hall — which is precisely what a town would talk about. *It's A Wipe!* has no town to hear anything; this is one of the four differentiators docs/00 §4.3 says must ship as ours.

**Canon:** raw notes: "Quest/achievement board" (Guildhall list); "the town will be improved by the raids you do. **Mostly** by gaining reputation levels" — mostly, not only. DECIDED/recorded: docs/15 Q-69's own default "Board yes, capped at ~15%"; docs/02 §4.4 ("the *only* non-mission reputation faucet proposed here"); docs/11 §11.2 "doc 03 §6 owns rates". Not in §9's binding six. DESIGNER-13's "no" rested on an unknown N moving every threshold; with N fixed the arithmetic answers it: 2 × 15 = 30 RP against a 3390-RP campaign (0.9%); the wall opens at Known (`Achievements.UNLOCK_RANK`, ≥120 RP), where the live cap is already ⌊0.15 × 120⌋ = 18 ≥ 15, so the first record is claimable the day the wall opens and can never fire before the first threshold; docs/03 §6.4's proof holds unchanged — 2815 + 30 + 325 = 3170 < 3200, so Legendary still lands on Raid 5's full-clear bonus. 15 RP is docs/03 §6.1's own smallest unit — the two tutorials together (A0 5 + Tutorial Raid 10), the amount §6.1 calls "deliberately trivial" to forfeit — so a record is worth a tutorial, never an adventure.

**Cost:** `data/achievements.json`: `rost_one_of_each` and `rost_comfortable` gain `"rp": 15` (their `_rp` provenance lines rewritten to cite this row and docs/03 §6.1's new line). `game/core/GameState.gd` `FLAG_DEFAULTS` gains `"achievement_rp": true` and `:2920` passes `flag("achievement_rp")` in place of the literal `false`. `sim/core/Achievements.gd` `claim_grant()` gains the reputation branch its coin branch already has — `rp_paid(claimed)` + `rp_cap(rp_earned_lifetime)` (`:801`, present and unwired) with the blocked sentence "The town has heard its share for now — %d of the %d reputation it may pay against %d earned." — and `describe_reward()`'s "(reputation is not signed off — …)" clause is deleted (it can no longer print). docs/03 §6.1 gains the row "Record wall, reputation-kind records | — | 15 | 0 (a record is claimed once)" and the cap line; docs/15 Q-69 closes (the time-of-day half is not this batch's). `tests/unit/test_achievements.gd`: claims pay 15, board RP ≤ 15% of `rp_earned_lifetime` over the scripted full run, the note never prints. Balance: +30 RP lifetime, earliest at Known; no rank arrives more than one encounter early; the pacing tests run the mission table and are untouched; LOOP-13's feed line then reads "The town heard: +15 reputation" on a claim. Unit: W7-DOCS (S: two data lines, one flag, one branch, one doc row). Note for CONTENT: the day-one twelve cover all nine classes (`StartingRoster.gd:34-35`), so "One of Each" is earned before the first raid whatever it pays; that is the record's condition, not the reward's problem.

**Register row:** Q-69 (board half) — The record wall pays reputation. Records of kind `reputation` (two ship: One of Each, Comfortable) pay 15 RP each — docs/03 §6.1's smallest award, the two tutorials' worth — claimed once, and the board's lifetime RP is capped live at 15% of RP earned exactly as its coin is (`Achievements.rp_cap`), so a claim past the share is blocked with a sentence rather than paid short. `FLAG_DEFAULTS.achievement_rp = true`; docs/03 §6.1 carries the row; audit M5-QAB-4 closes. **Inspiration:** The designer wrote training as a conditional, not a feature: "Train raiders < Maybe if we have level ups". The "Lv. 12" on the reference cards is the concept artist's placeholder for a stat the design does not have, and the standing rule is that where the reference's UI does not fit the design, the design wins (memory: reference-concepts-are-a-standard). A card that can print a stat the game never sets is a second truth waiting for a fixture to expose it — which is exactly what happened (`_sheet_1.png` "Bork Lv. 12").

**Canon:** raw notes: "Train raiders < Maybe if we have level ups"; the recruit model is rarity only ("Common raiders … uncommon raiders … rare raiders … epic raiders … Legendary raiders") and gear is the only stat source ("Tier 1 Adventure gear in screenshots").
