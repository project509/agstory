## 2. The decision that outranks everything else

### `M6-BAL-04` — the Tier 1 campaign is not completable, and it is measured rather than suspected

`./tools/with_godot_lock.sh "$GODOT" --headless --path . --script res://tools/playtest.gd` plays eight
fresh guilds through the whole of Tier 1. **None of them finish.** Six stop at Adventure 2, two at
Adventure 1.

The cause is a closed circuit, and every link in it is canon:

1. A new guild is twelve Commons. `docs/05` §7.5 puts a Common's morale baseline at **45** (`50` plus a
   `−5` rarity offset).
2. `docs/05` §8's drift stops dead at the baseline, so `rest_until_recovered()` can never return a roster
   above 45 while the guild is at facility tier 0.
3. At morale 45, in full Tier 1 Adventure gear, A2 is a wall. The sweep reads the same thing from the
   other direction: **morale moves clear rate by 100 percentage points, rarity by 13.** Morale is not one
   lever among several — it is *the* lever.
4. No clears means no gold, no gold means no facility, no facility means no morale. The thing that would
   raise the baseline has to be bought with the winnings the baseline prevents.

**Four levers, all of them yours.** Each changes a number that has already shipped, which is precisely
why the loop filed this instead of fixing it:

| | Lever | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | Raise the Common morale baseline (`docs/05` §7.5's `−5` offset) | Every band reading in `docs/05` §8 moves, and the morale history's whole scale with it |
| **b** | Make the first facility reachable — `docs/11` §8.2's Guildhall price against `docs/01` §8.0's 60 G opening purse | A guild that can afford tier 1 immediately starts at the morale the ladder appears to have been sized for |
| **c** | Size A1–A3 for 45 | Admits the on-ramp was costed against a morale the player cannot reach. `docs/08` §9's budget is the instrument |
| **d** | Change what a wipe costs in morale (`docs/05` §7) | It is the wipe delta that drags a roster to the floor between attempts |

**Please do not split the difference**, and nothing in the harness should be softened to make the
playtest green. Record the ruling in `docs/15` and re-run `playtest.gd` — it is now the instrument that
says whether it worked.

## 3. The two pinned defects

`docs/15` records exactly one number ever changed on judgement alone (BL-29), and the bar is that there
stays one. So both of these are held by tests that assert the **measured** failure, which makes fixing
them a visible, deliberate act rather than a nudge:

- **Adventure 3 is a wall.** `test_adventures.gd` pins a 0.0 clear rate. Measured: 24/24 clears with no
  mechanics, 0/24 with m01 alone, 3/24 with m02, 9/24 with m03.
- **The Tutorial Raid is unwinnable.** `test_tutorials.gd` pins 0 clears in 20. As authored 0/20; without
  m01, 13/20; with the boss swing cut 15 → 10, 10/20.

**Both trace to one cause:** canon prices a boss swing against the autoattack alone, and then adds M01,
which multiplies it by 2.5. That is one ruling (`M6-BAL-03`), not two fixes.

## 5. What shipped this session — 47 commits

Grouped by what it was for. Every one is gated and has tests.

**The screens came off the mockups (your art directive).** `SceneStage` gained an actor layer;
`tools/art/gen_actors.py` turns the reference's own sliced idle frames into 56 animation strips. Six
stage scenes are authored; nine of fifteen screens now stand on a bare plate with our own figures
breathing over it — Town, Tavern, Market, RaidPrep, RaidView, Results, AdventureBoard, MainMenu and the
new Completion screen. Two of those were standing in the *wrong room* and nobody had noticed: the Market
was showing the camp, the Adventure Board was showing the hall. The twelve combatants are on the arena
floor in measured positions, the boss is a sprite that greys when its bar empties, canon's armour
families decide which figure is which class (`docs/15` BL-68), and the water and crystals move.

**The endgame exists and is reachable.** The ending fires, S17 was built to the row `docs/13` gained from
`docs/10`, the achievement board got its first caller after weeks of being complete and invisible, and
the save format froze at v16 with its migration chain walked by a test.

**Tier 1 was reconciled to its own budget.** `docs/08` §9's boss budget was derived for tiers 2–5 by the
same method that reproduces Tier 1's hand-published numbers; running the generator found two real
defects (a generated rung inverted at the slot level, and 336 items called "TIER2's Boots" had shipped
into the live content set); three trinkets in four could never drop; and Tier 1's shipped numbers turned
out to be two corrections behind §9.2.

**Accessibility became gates rather than intentions.** Keyboard access is a verify stage, and so is a
motion lint — reduced motion, the 3-changes-a-second flashing rule, and emoji-free mode each go through
one door now, with both readings of the ambiguous ones switched and tested.

**Five instruments were found complete and never called**, which is the pattern of the week:
`Achievements.evaluate()`, `tools/export_build.sh`, `gen_items.gd -- check`, `Cards.morale_glyph()`, and
the balance drift gate, whose baseline had never been generated — so it answered "NO BASELINE" and gated
nothing. All five are armed, and each has a test asserting it stays callable.

**The queue learned to tell the truth.** Sixteen open audit items turned out to be finished; the work had
landed inside other items and nobody walked back to the plan, and on two consecutive iterations the loop
picked something already done. `tools/audit_stale.py` now ranks every open item by how much of what it
names already exists in the tree.

**The campaign got played.** `tools/playtest.gd` walks the whole of Tier 1 and reports — which is how §2
stopped being a suspicion.

## 6. What is honest about the state

- **The export gate is green and deliberately held.** `./tools/export_build.sh` produces a shippable
  build except that it refuses on 16 files carrying `stats_pending` / `name_pending`: the eight generated
  tier tables (unnamed, BL-69) and the eight Legendaries `docs/03` §5.6 forbids naming. **That gate
  clears when you supply names, and never by editing the gate.** `--dev` makes a playable build stamped
  NOT SHIPPABLE.
- **The working copy is still on OneDrive.** `docs/15` Q-18 recommends moving it: sync plus Godot's
  generated `.godot/` cache can produce file locks and import corruption. `.godot/` is gitignored and
  regenerated and `verify.sh` would catch breakage, so the loop treated it as an accepted risk rather
  than relocating your project directory unilaterally. **It is still your call.**
- **The balance baseline records what the game IS, not what it should be.** `tests/baselines/sweep_baseline.csv`
  is 4,000 runs: A1 clears 34.2% for Commons at morale 55 in starting gear, A2 99.8% and A3 98.2% in
  Adventure gear, E1–E4 100% in raid-entry gear, E5 75.4%. Any balance ruling you make will move these,
  and `--rebaseline` exists so that move is a reviewed diff with a receipt. **It must never be run to
  make a red gate green.**
- **`sim/` is pure and the UI is a log player.** No presentation has been allowed to outrun the
  simulation, and the goldens hold that.
- **80 audit items have not been started.** The loop was picking one per iteration and finishing it
  properly; it was not trying to close the queue.

## 7. How to look at it yourself

```bash
./tools/run_game.sh                                   # play it, windowed
./tools/with_godot_lock.sh ./tools/verify.sh          # the 8-stage gate, ~3 minutes
./tools/with_godot_lock.sh "$GODOT" --headless --path . --script res://tools/playtest.gd
./tools/diff_all.sh                                   # every converted screen against its concept
python tools/audit_stale.py --top 15                  # open items whose work may already exist
```

**Every Godot invocation goes through `tools/with_godot_lock.sh`.** Two engine processes in this project
corrupt `.godot/` and `user://`, and the symptom is a *save* test failing in a run that never touched
saves.

**Where to read next:** `BUILD_STATE.md` is the loop's memory and is current. `BACKLOG.md` is the forward
list. `build/plan/audit.json` is the queue, and every item in §4 above is in it with its options costed.
`LESSONS.md` holds the traps that each cost time twice. `docs/15-open-questions.md` is the decision
register — §9, "Answer these six first", is still the fastest half-hour you can spend on this project.
