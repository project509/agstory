## Judgement calls

- J1 (the verbs run on the stage clock, not on `Widgets.tween`): the plan's Findings say "all through Widgets.tween with motion_duration and holds as floor_ms", and its Acceptance says the verb tests go "through from_data". Both cannot hold: the unit suite runs from `_initialize`, nothing is inside a tree (test_a11y.gd:756 pins it), `Widgets.tween` answers null there by its own contract, and a Tween cannot be stepped by hand — so a Widgets.tween `hit` would make `test_hit_recoils_and_returns` an assertion of nothing. RULES §2 (the law where a Fix disagrees) puts world-layer motion — AnimatedSprite2Ds, particles, shaders — behind `SceneStage.apply_settings`, not the UI's tween door, and the stage already drives its bob, pulses, flicker and held speech from `_process`. So the acts are clock-driven keyframes; `_beat(ms, floor_ms)` is `GameSettings.motion_duration`'s exact arithmetic (max(floor, ms * scale)) with the scale being the stage's held flag, flourishes' distances and angles are multiplied by `_motion_scale()` (the wipe stamp's tilt rule), nothing branches on the setting, and `create_tween` stays at zero in this file (lint). W3-RAIDVIEW2 gets a handle-free API (a new verb on a busy figure replaces the act; `_end_act_on` is the kill).
