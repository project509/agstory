## Judgement calls (rule 7)

- J1 `Icons.at("ui", "blot_12_a")` / `("ui","lock_16")` in the plan are shorthand: `Icons.gd` (W1-ICONS) has roles `blot` (key a|b|c) and `lock` (key 16). The screen uses `Icons.at("blot","a")` and `Icons.at("lock","16")`.
- J2 The pager: the plan's "via `Cards.roster_strip`'s built-in paging" does not apply — RaidView does not draw a roster strip (its four combatant panels are its own). W1-KIT's API note names `Widgets.pager(page, pages, on_prev, on_next, caption)` as "the captioned row for RaidView"; that is what lands.
- J3 The status row's morale chip: `Widgets.chip` is a 96x45 header chip and does not fit the 28px band at panel-local y 121..149, so the row uses the chip's LABEL door — `LabelMorale` (the tabular face font) — with `Cards.morale_glyph(self, m)` so emoji-free still lands its pips in `Label.text`.
- J4 The skip reason sits ABOVE its button: `Widgets.reasoned` puts the Reason Label under the control, and under the control row (y 675..720) there are 15px before the strip plate at 735. The Reason is moved to index 0; the box is bottom-aligned to the row.
- J5 The "!" over a downed figure: docs/12's `#F2C14E` has no Palette token; the Label uses `Palette.CAUTION`. The hit flash uses `Palette.TEXT_BODY` and the handoff asks for `Palette.HIT_FLASH`.
- J6 The wipe stamp's rest angle is `stamp_box`'s own lean; the press starts at lean + `WIPE_STAMP_TILT_DEG * motion_scale()` and tweens to the lean, so under reduced motion there is no rotation MOTION while the static lean stays (W1-KIT review M7).
- J7 G08's coalescing: at Speed.ONE the rim/brighten/mech-cell effects fire per line; at Speed.TWO once per round; at FOUR/INSTANT (and `_on_skip`) they are off. Numbers, bubbles and bars are world-layer (BL-75) and fire at every speed.
- J8 (repair, F1) The bar keeps its explicit placement rather than an HBox (kit Containers re-lay positioned children — the +14 sidebar drift lesson); `_place_in_bar` reads the button's width back after sizing (a Control clamps `size` to its minimum), so a button whose text outgrows its slot pushes its neighbours instead of being painted over. The slots themselves scale with the text so the row keeps its 100% proportions.
- J9 (repair, F6) A MISTAKE row's first Label is the stamp's word "MISTAKE" and the header is the row's LAST child; test_log_player reads RaidView rows by `contains` and its first-text assertion is on Results' `_story_row`; my own test pins the header as the last child.
