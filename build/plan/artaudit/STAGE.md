## Summary

The stage is a real, data-driven layer (plate, emissive flames, breathing lights, embers, water shimmer, crystal pulse, 56 idle strips, bubbles, a bob-only boss) and all six bare-plate scenes exist — but what is on it is still at the wrong scale and in the wrong place, and it has no verbs. Three P0s: the raid's twelve figures stand as a 40px row on the far upper ledge with the boss tucked under its own HP plate, so the fight does not read as one (STAGE-01); every placed figure on camp and the arenas is 1x where the plates' own objects and the reference's 81-104px bodies say 2x (STAGE-02); the stage has no hit / burst / bolt / slash API, so the log plays over an inert floor (STAGE-03). Behind them: no numbers, over-head bars or in-fight bubble (04); the crystal pulse quads are visible hard rectangles on RaidPrep/RaidView/Results (05); the dungeon arena's geyser is authored off the band the screens show and the plate has never been on screen (06); no DoF/vignette/fore/grade (07); single-frame bosses (08) and idle-only raiders with no fumble (09); a tail-less speaking bubble with no speaker (10). Two dead scene files still target mockup crops with painted people, and the hall family still loads guildhall_plate.png although today's message puts it on the camp (13). Concrete per-plate blocking rects and figure scales for both arenas are given in "Proposed arena blocking". Nine designer questions are listed; none of the P0 fixes needs one to start, only to sign off.

## Findings

### STAGE-01 · P0 · composition · The raid does not read as a fight: twelve 40px figures on the far upper ledge, the boss tucked under its own HP plate
Evidence: RaidView.gd:47 `ARENA_OFFSET (0,-280)`; :63-67 `PARTY_RANKS` put the ranks at plate y 374..414, x 620..846 -> screen (620,94)-(846,134), a 40px-tall row wedged between the wordmark header and the boss plate — shot RaidView (620,60,230,80). Boss: :76 `BOSS_X 1190`, :86-93 feet = 404+h clamped 481..556 -> boss_main (99x119) feet at plate 523 = screen 243, head at 124, i.e. 6px under the boss plate's bottom edge — shot RaidView (1140,120,110,125). The reference fight occupies the middle band: party feet at y 521-660 with 81-104px bodies (spec 02:258-268), boss body 730..1310 x 110..700 (spec 02:270), Concept 2 (200,380,1100,300).
Why it matters: this is the screen the player watches for minutes; the directive today names combat first. The plate's plateau is used as a shelf for a row of miniatures and the enemy is a sticker beside the chrome — nothing on the floor is where the eye goes.
Fix: move the blocking out of RaidView's constants and into the arena scene JSON as `marks` (`{"view": [0,-280], "party": {"ranks": [{"y":692,"xs":[468,532,596,660]}, ...], "scale": 2, "face": "right"}, "boss": {"pos": [1210,800], "scale": 2.5, "flip": true, "floor": [1090,730,230,120]}}`), read through a static `SceneStage.marks(name)`; RaidView `_party_layout()` and `_stage()` consume them; `boss_feet_y()` becomes "feet inside the mark's floor rect, head >= view-relative chrome clearance". Numbers per plate in "Proposed arena blocking" below.
Assets: none new (the strips and slices exist). preview_scene.py gains `--marks` to draw the rects and a 2x figure at each mark (STAGE-18).
Acceptance: fixture shot RaidView: every party sprite's feet inside the party rect, figure heights >= 70px, boss head y >= 124 and feet inside its floor rect, no sprite under BOSS_PLATE (931,53,384,65) or OBJECTIVE (0,88,352,106). tests/unit/test_scene_stage.gd::test_every_creature_the_sheet_can_produce_clears_the_chrome_and_lands_on_stone (:534-566) is rewritten to iterate both arena scenes' marks instead of hard-coding plate y 481..556. test_screens.gd reads Labels/Buttons only — untouched.
Needs designer: no for the mechanism; yes for the final composition sign-off (the two candidate rects are given; the choice between "party lower-left, boss lower-right across the bridge" and "party on the plateau" is an art call).
Verdict: CONFIRMED — RaidView.gd:47/:63-67/:76/:86-93/:94-100 hold the cited constants (PARTY_RANKS y 374/394/414 -> screen 94..134; boss_main feet 523 -> head screen 124, 6px under BOSS_PLATE's bottom 118) and the RaidView shot regions show a 40px row on the upper plateau and the boss under the plate's icon row, against Concept 2's mid-band fight; only nit: the ranks' xs start at 630, not 620.

### STAGE-03 · P0 · missing-widget · The stage has no combat verbs: no hit recoil/flash, no impact burst, no bolt, no slash — the log plays and the floor stays inert
Evidence: SceneStage.gd has no one-shot API (grep flash|spark|bolt|burst -> none); RaidView's only stage reactions are tints — `_refresh_party_sprites` (:860-871) and the boss grey at zero (:901-903); `_append_line` (:1238-1275) writes rows only. spec 09 §4.3 row 6 "VFX (bolts, slashes, hit sparks) — GPUParticles2D / frame sprites"; spec 02 §9.1 measures five VFX with 5-stop ramps (arcane orb 50x57, muzzle flash 75x58, red impact burst 160x160, cyan slash arc 125x105, void lightning); docs/12 §5.2 `hit` = 2px recoil + 1 frame `#FFF6DC` flash; docs/13 §11 "the player is watching, not playing. This screen either carries the comedy or wastes it".
Why it matters: the reference frame is a fight mid-swing (Concept 2 (560,400,500,260)); ours is twelve people standing still while text scrolls. The directive's "combat UI designed beautifully" cannot be met by chrome alone.
Fix: SceneStage gains four stage verbs, all data-free and all going through `Widgets.tween()` (tools/lint_motion.sh) with durations from `GameSettings.motion_duration()`: `hit(spr)` — 2px recoil away from the attacker + modulate to #FFF6DC for 90ms then back; `burst(pos, kind)` — a one-shot GPUParticles2D (`one_shot`, `explosiveness 0.9`, 12-16 particles, 0.35s) whose colour ramp is the spec 02 §9.1 row for `kind` in {arcane, fire, impact, slash, void}; `bolt(from, to, kind)` — a Sprite2D or Line2D travelling 180ms with a 3-frame trail; `slash(pos)` — a 4-frame 48x40 arc strip. RaidView `_append_line` maps `Enums.Verb` ATTACK -> bolt/slash + hit + burst on the target, HEAL -> a green burst on the target, MISTAKE -> the fumble beat (STAGE-09). Reduced motion: hit = flash only (no recoil), bolt = burst at the target with no travel; reduced flashing: GameSettings' 3-changes/s cap — one flash per entry, entries arrive slower than that at 1x.
Assets: tools/aseprite/gen_fx.lua -> game/assets/vfx/fx_spark.png (6 frames 16x16), fx_slash.png (4 frames 48x40), fx_bolt_arcane.png / fx_bolt_fire.png (12x6 with trail), fx_impact.png (5 frames 48x48) — palette from spec 02 §9.1 ramps; sources art/src/vfx/fx_*.aseprite. Particle textures can reuse ember.png with per-emitter ramps.
Acceptance: `godot --script tools/shot.gd -- RaidView --fixture=raid` at 3s shows at least one burst or number over the boss; new tests: test_hit_recoils_and_returns, test_burst_is_one_shot_and_frees_itself, test_reduced_motion_makes_hit_a_flash_only (position unchanged), all through `from_data`.
Needs designer: no (the spec rows are measured); which verb maps to which class ability is data the sim already emits.
Verdict: CONFIRMED — grep flash|spark|bolt|burst over SceneStage.gd is empty, RaidView.gd:860-873 and :901-903 only set modulate, :1238-1276 only builds rows, spec 09 §4.3 row 6, spec 02 §9.1's five measured VFX, docs/12 §5.2 `hit` and docs/13:600 read as cited, and `Enums.Verb {ATTACK, HEAL, MECHANIC, MISTAKE, …}` exists (sim/model/Enums.gd:173); fix notes: docs/15 BL-75 is DECIDED 'UI only', so the 3-changes/s hedge is moot for stage flashes, but a one-shot GPUParticles2D `burst` is still motion and should be held (single frame or skipped) under reduced_motion, not merely re-targeted.
