## Summary

The town family is fully on bare plates: no baked figures, bubbles or label pills remain (the camp's people, the hearth fire and the ellipsis bubbles are all stage-rendered), so the 2026-09-11 rule is met here. What is not met is the "actual working graphics" half of today's note: the five building callouts are flat text boxes instead of spec 03 §3's iconed, chamfered, tailed signposts (TOWN-01); the camp's speech line has no speaker or tail (TOWN-03); the Town sidebar is a help page where spec 10 §3.1 puts the next-mission panel (TOWN-06); the Market is text buttons where the slot grid was planned (TOWN-21); candidate cards omit the canon-required arriving gear (TOWN-18); and nothing on the hub ever changes with rank or building level (TOWN-15). Two structural defects sit under everything: the project's own memory assigns the hub's plate two different ways (TOWN-14, needs a ruling before the hall family converts), and the desk strip shows 4 of 12 raiders with no pager on three screens (TOWN-16). Layout bugs a player sees at once: the rail label overflowing on every screen (TOWN-04), the Tavern sidebar spilling into the desk band (TOWN-17), the Day-chip icon and wordmark size changing between screens (TOWN-05), clipped list/link edges on the Board (TOWN-10). 29 findings: 2 P0, 10 P1, 12 P2, 5 P3; six need a designer answer, the rest are buildable from the specs as written.

### TOWN-01 · P0 · missing-widget · Building callouts are flat text boxes, not Concept 3's signposts
Evidence: Town.png (225,293,230,90) Guildhall, (641,191,230,90) Tavern, (902,293,238,125) Blacksmith, (861,415,230,95) Market, (421,581,230,90) Adventure's Board — a dark box, a bold name, a small verb, nothing else. Hover per docs/13 §12.3: plate one step lighter + 1px lift, no scale, no pulse; focus: the 2px brass ring outside the bounds. Keep the Button as the callout's title (Widgets.gd:476-487) so `Widgets.button_of()` and the test presses still work; name/verb/reason stay Labels.
Assets: five 24px building glyphs (guild banner, tankard, scales, anvil, notice board) — none exist on the reference sheets (spec 07's catalogue has only an "anvil_table" prop), so add them to tools/art/gen_icons.lua (span-authored, same treatment as the rank sigils); the chamfered callout 9-slice via tools/aseprite/gen_ui.lua.
Acceptance: Town.png shows five chamfered, iconed callouts each with a tail touching its building; a11y_smoke's focus pass shows the ring; tests/unit/test_screens.gd:240-312 still reads "Guildhall", "Tavern", "Market", "Adventure's Board", "maybe".
Needs designer: no — spec 03 §3 already decided the tail and the geometry.
Verdict: CONFIRMED — Town.png shows five flat plates with no icon or tail at the cited rects, Town.gd:158-167 passes only name/verb/reason, Widgets.gd:468-502 builds a PanelCallout (Theme.gd:229: 1px rounded StyleBoxFlat) with no icon or pointer, and spec 03 §3 / Concept 3 (392,112) show the iconed, chamfered, tailed plate; the fix keeps the title Button, which is what test_screens.gd:309-311 reads ("Guildhall"…"maybe" via Label/Button text).
