## Summary

The combat surface is structurally right and visually empty. RaidView stands on the bare cave plate (no baked figures — the 2026-09-11 directive is met for the plate itself), its chrome follows spec 02's measured rects, the HP fold and the wipe clock are correct and tested. But the fight is not the picture: the twelve are a 40px cluster on the far ledge and the boss a 100px slice, nothing on the floor can act, and none of spec 02 §5/§9's world-space layer exists — no damage numbers, no floating bars, no bubbles, no sparks, no stamps, no blots. Results fits its fallen grid now but its post-mortem lacks the by-raider tally the design specifies, its cards carry system emoji, and its right column is dead on the common (wipe) outcome. Three P0s (composition, numbers, overlays), eight P1s, six P2s, three P3s; twenty findings, six of which need a designer ruling before the code moves.

## Findings

### COMBAT-01 · P0 · composition · The battle line is a 40px cluster on the far ledge and the boss is a 100px head — the fight is not the picture
Evidence: game/screens/RaidView.gd:47 `ARENA_OFFSET := Vector2(0, -280)`; :63-67 `PARTY_RANKS` y 374/394/414 in plate space → screen y 94..134; :76 `BOSS_X := 1190`, :86-93 `boss_feet_y()` clamps feet to plate 481..556 → screen 201..276. Actor strips (game/assets/actors/actors.json) are 26-39px wide, 37-48px tall at scale 1.0 (RaidView never passes `scale`; SceneStage.gd:197 default 1.0). Boss textures (game/assets/enemies/) are 83x81, 99x119, 150x104, 103x93, 111x152, 36x77. Shot RaidView.png: twelve figures at x 620-830, y 40-120; boss at x 1150-1240, y 120-240, floating in front of the mine-cart gallery with its feet on nothing readable. Reference Concept 2: sprites 58-75 wide / 81-104 tall at 1:1 with feet at y 521-668 (spec 02 §7 table), boss body 730..1310 x 110..700, battle line mid-frame. The foreground of our plate (screen x 200-1100, y 240-420 plateau; y 560-700 pool edge) is empty.
Why it matters: docs/13 §11 — "the player is watching, not playing. This screen either carries the comedy or wastes it." A fight the eye cannot find wastes it; the designer's directive asks for combat UI "designed beautifully" and this is the first thing they will see. The plate's perspective makes higher = farther, so the party placed at plate y 374-414 reads as background extras, and the boss at 1:1 from a 99px slice is a fifth of the reference's mass.
Fix: (a) move the fight into the lower-middle third: ranks on the near plateau (screen y ~330-420, plate 610-700, x 300-700) or on the bridge-side floor, boss on the right gallery floor with feet at screen y ~480-560 (plate 760-840), facing left; (b) scale actors x2 (SceneStage `scale` is already supported per actor — pass `"scale": 2` in `_party_layout()`; nearest filter keeps pixels square) and the boss x3-x4 (or author a real boss at 2D-HD size, see COMBAT-07); (c) re-measure the plate rows with tools/art/refkit.py so the feet land on lit stone the way the current docstring did for the wrong rows. Keep depth sort (SceneStage `_sorted_actors`) so the back rank overlaps correctly at the larger size. `PARTY_RANKS` should become a wedge (x pitch 90-120, y pitch 40-60, tanks front-right) rather than three straight rows of four, per spec 02 §7 "loose staggered wedge".
Assets: none for the first pass (scale existing strips); a 2D-HD boss sheet later (COMBAT-07).
Acceptance: new RaidView shot shows the twelve at ≥74px tall with feet on visible stone in y 330-560, the boss ≥300px tall on the right gallery, nothing under the boss plate chrome; tests/unit/test_scene_stage.gd's formation assertions updated to the new rects (they read `RaidView.PARTY_RANKS` / `boss_feet_y`).
Needs designer: partly — which floor is the battle line (near plateau vs bridge approach) is a composition call the designer may want to make from two candidate shots; the scale change is not.
Verdict: CONFIRMED — RaidView.gd:47/63-67/76/86-93, actors.json and the enemies/ dims read as cited and the shot shows the twelve at x 620-830, y 40-120 with the boss at x 1150-1240; two corrections: the tests read only `boss_feet_y` (test_scene_stage.gd:552, with the 398/481/556 plate rows hard-coded as literals that a floor move must edit), not `PARTY_RANKS`, and the class-mapped strips run 25-39 wide / 38-48 tall (rogue is 25x38).

### COMBAT-02 · P0 · missing-widget · Floating damage and heal numbers do not exist; Type.DAMAGE / DAMAGE_CRIT / Palette.CRIT have zero consumers
Evidence: build/plan/audit.json M6-JUICE-05 `actual_status: "not-started"`; `grep -rn "DAMAGE_CRIT\|Type.DAMAGE\|Palette.CRIT" game/` finds only the definitions (game/ui/Type.gd, game/ui/Palette.gd). Reference: "-842" crit (orange, 22px digits, 2px outline) and "-317" (white, 16px) at spec 02 §5.4. Shot RaidView.png: no number anywhere over the arena.
Why it matters: docs/13 §11.3's two-second glance — "who is hurt and by how much" is answered only by reading the log row and then finding the right paged panel. The reference's most legible combat signal is absent.
Fix: `Widgets.damage_number(amount, kind) -> Label` (styles `LabelDamage`, `LabelDamageCrit`, `LabelHeal` in game/ui/Theme.gd; Fonts.ui_tabular(600) — no custom draw, so test_screens' Label.text harvest still sees them), spawned from `_append_line` for `Enums.Verb.ATTACK`/`HEAL` entries with an amount, anchored at the target sprite's head (`_party_sprites[id].position - (0, frame_h)` in stage space + stage.position) or the boss anchor; rise 18px and fade over 600ms through `Widgets.tween()` with `GameSettings.motion_duration(600)` so tools/lint_motion.sh passes; under reduced_motion hold 600ms then free. Value is set once (docs/13 §12.2 "numbers are instant, never tweened in value"). Stagger simultaneous numbers by +14px y so a raid-wide hit on twelve does not stack into one blob. Colour: raider damage `Palette.DANGER`, crit `Palette.CRIT`, heal `#83DF83`-equivalent `Palette.POSITIVE`, boss damage white.
Assets: none (font glyphs with outline via `LabelSettings.outline_size = 2`).
Acceptance: a shot mid-fight (fixture with the player advanced ~10 entries) shows a number over the struck combatant; tests/unit/test_motion.gd asserts the number's value text never changes after spawn and that reduced_motion yields a zero-distance path.
Needs designer: no.
Verdict: CONFIRMED — Type.gd:14-15 (DAMAGE_CRIT, DAMAGE) and Palette.gd:77 (CRIT) are the only hits for those tokens under game/, audit M6-JUICE-05 is `not-started`, `_append_line` spawns nothing on the stage and the shot carries no number; the Fix passes tools/lint_motion.sh (it gates only direct `create_tween(` outside Widgets.gd) and `Fonts.ui_tabular` exists (Fonts.gd:44).

### COMBAT-03 · P0 · missing-widget · No world-space overlays: no floating status bar, no "!" downed badge, no in-combat speech bubble, no hit spark or spell VFX
Evidence: spec 02 §5.1-5.3 and §9.1 specify per-sprite HP bar + class badge + pip row, the 22x22 "!" plate on a downed raider, the chamfered speech bubble with tail, and four spell VFX ramps. RaidView.gd has no code that creates any Control anchored to a sprite: the only sprite-level reaction is `_refresh_party_sprites()` (:860-873) tinting a downed/dead figure grey and stopping its idle. game/assets/scenes/stage_arena_cave.json declares no `bubbles`/`speech` (props, lights, embers, shimmer, pulse only), so SceneStage's bubble layer is idle on this stage. game/assets/vfx/ holds only fire strips, ember.png and light_soft.png — no spark, slash, orb, burst. The jokes exist (`e.joke()` at RaidView.gd:1269) but go only into the log.
Why it matters: this is the directive's "actual working graphics of the game" — the arena currently animates lanterns and water but nothing about the fight itself; a wipe is twelve figures going grey. The mistake joke is the game's comedy and it lands only as an indented log line, not on the character who made it.
Fix, in three widgets under game/ui/ (or a new game/ui/Overlays.gd owned by RaidView): (1) `FloatingBar`: 62x7 HP bar + 24x24 class badge + up to 4 12px pips, screen-space Control positioned each frame from the sprite's head anchor, refreshed from the same `_raider_hp/_raider_state` fold; the badge swaps to the "!" plate when state == downed/dead. (2) `CombatBubble`: reuse `Widgets.speech_plate()` with a tail (spec §5.3 chamfer, 117x56 min), shown for ~2.4s at `_append_line` when `e.joke()` is non-empty, above the actor's head; body text is a Label so tests can read it. (3) VFX: an `AnimatedSprite2D` one-shot layer on the stage — `SceneStage.play_fx(strip, at)`; four strips authored via tools/aseprite/ Lua (impact burst 48x48x6, slash arc 64x48x5, arcane orb 32x32x6, heal sparkle 32x32x6) using the ramps in spec 02 §9.1; fired from `_append_line` on ATTACK (burst at target), HEAL (sparkle at target), MECHANIC (boss eye flash). All under `GameSettings.reduced_effects` (drop VFX) and `reduced_motion` (bubbles appear/disappear without tween).
Assets: game/assets/vfx/fx_impact.png, fx_slash.png, fx_orb.png, fx_heal.png (+ .aseprite sources), game/assets/ui/icons/badge_downed.png (22x22 "!" plate), class badge 24x24 x9 (or reuse icons/class_*.png — only class_warrior.png exists today).
Acceptance: shot mid-fight shows a bar above each of the twelve, a bubble over the mistake-maker with the joke text, and a burst frame on the struck target; tests/unit/test_screens.gd's Label.text harvest includes the bubble text; tools/lint_motion.sh clean.
Needs designer: only for the bubble's copy length (does the joke go in the bubble in full, or a short "bark" with the full line in the log? docs/13 §11.3 implies the log carries the full quote).
Verdict: CONFIRMED — stage_arena_cave.json ships `bubbles: []` and no `speech` key, game/assets/vfx holds only ember/fire_*/light_soft, RaidView.gd:860-873 is the only sprite-level reaction, and the joke at :1269 goes only into the log column.
