## Judgement calls

- The band line's ladder is per card (the size drops only where the line needs it), not per scale: 88 of the 90 class/band lines keep the variation's 15px at 100%, byte-identical to before; the two Shaman lines go to 13. A uniform drop would have moved every card for two lines.
- The wrapped rung (125/150 only) shapes on the plain Fira face at CLASS-2, not the variation's face at CLASS: the variation's line is the face sheet's (30/36) and this line never draws a face, so two of them were 20px of air per card; and — found the hard way — the variation must not be drawn at a size it was not built for at all (the log, 12:30-12:55).
- "Size the band to its content" is read as: the button plate hugs its 13px label (`ButtonCardAction`, 6px margins, the 9-slice's 14px corners making 28 the floor), the reason is the caption size flush under it, and a banded card closes its gaps to 2. Even so a reasoned band needs 7 more than the pad leaves, so the bottom pad goes to 7 for that card only (sides and top stay 14, as 01 §3 measured). The alternative — the reason drawn over the pad, which is what happened before — is what the review called the defect.
- `reasoned`'s "beside" puts the reason to the RIGHT of the control on one row; Settings' table (control in the last column) and LoadSave's once-per-phrase row will still want their own placement — the kit form is there for a row that has the room, and both screens adopt later by the contract.
- `pips` counts `steps` pips (default 6, the contract's number) lit at `maximum * i / steps` and NAMES them by threshold, so `pips(level, 100, 5)` reproduces Settings' five audible steps and its "Pip20".."Pip100" names exactly; Settings' 0 is the empty strip either way.
- `event_log`'s default `rows` is `ROWS` now (was 7, capped to 6 inside): identical output, and the constant is the number a screen quotes.
- `ButtonCardAction` is a new Button-family variation (through `_button()`, ring and all); `ButtonMiniPicked` keeps Facilities' rim without the 1px pressed drop — a held toggle is a state.
